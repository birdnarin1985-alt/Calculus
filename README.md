## 🌟 Overview

**Siam Math Lab** is a high-fidelity, web-based educational simulator designed to bridge the gap between abstract mathematical formulas and intuitive 3D visualization. Built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and high-performance SVG graphics, this project requires **zero installations** and runs instantly inside any mobile or desktop browser.

Originally conceptualized as a national educational asset and global open resource, this tool empowers students and educators to explore multivariable calculus, differential geometry, and vector fields interactively.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🔄 6 Interactive 3D Mathematical Models:**
  1. *Solid of Revolution & Riemann Sum (Integral Calculus)*
  2. *Differential Involute Curves & Tangents (Differential Geometry)*
  3. *3D Harmonic Waveforms & Derivatives (Trigonometric Analysis)*
  4. *Orthogonal Basis & Normal Planes (Multivariable Vector Calculus)*
  5. *Doubly-Ruled Hyperboloids & Partial Traces (Quadric Surfaces)*
  6. *Toroidal Flux & Stokes' Circulation (Vector Field Manifolds)*

* **🌐 Instant Bilingual Support (`TH | EN`):** Switch seamlessly between Thai and academic English technical terms with a single tap.
* **🎨 4 Academic Color Palettes:** Choose between *Cyber Neon*, *Chalkboard Classic*, *RGB-Axis Encoding*, and *Paper Blueprint* modes.
* **🤖 AI Prompt Lab:** Dynamically generates context-aware, difficulty-tiered prompts (Basic Intuitive vs. Rigorous Proof) for students to query LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) for deep exploration.
* **📰 Editorial Magazine A4 Print Engine:** Generates a strict, 1-page landscape A4 monograph layout complete with mathematical theorems, wireframe figures, and research inquiries ready for classroom distribution.
* **📱 Mobile-First Touch Controls:** Fluid 360-degree orbit rotation, perspective elevation tilt, and real-time parameter scaling optimized for iOS Safari and Android devices.
